Power & energy

UPS systems, battery banks and renewable setups, with installation, preventive maintenance, battery replacement and annual service contracts.

Online UPS. Conditions the supply continuously and carries the load through an outage until generators start or systems shut down cleanly.

VRLA batteries. Sealed lead-acid. Lower upfront cost and the long-standing default for UPS strings, but service life drops fast in hot plant rooms.

Lithium-ion. Smaller footprint, better heat tolerance and a longer service life than lead-acid, at a higher purchase price.

Renewables. Solar-assisted supply to offset grid draw at sites with the roof or land to use.

Why one supplier for IT and power.

When power and IT are bought separately, the gap between the two contracts is where outages start.

Separate IT and power contractors

  • Two contracts, two support numbers, two site visits.
  • UPS sized from a nameplate guess, not the rack load.
  • After an outage, each side points at the other.
  • Battery replacement falls between the two contracts.

JIO Technologies

  • One contract and one number for every layer.
  • UPS and battery runtime sized from the equipment we install.
  • One team owns the fault from the mains to the firewall.
  • Battery health is part of the same maintenance schedule.
