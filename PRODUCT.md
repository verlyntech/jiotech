# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack
WordPress theme. The live site (jiotechuae.com) runs WordPress 6.9, and the team keeps editing content in WordPress, so the redesign ships as a theme rather than a separate static site.

## Users
Primary: enterprise and government buyers in the UAE (IT, facilities and procurement leads at government bodies, banks, healthcare providers, construction and engineering firms, utilities) evaluating a long-term managed IT and power partner. Reseller and trade buyers are a separate audience served by the B2B portal at jiotech.ae. SMEs are not the primary target.

## Product Purpose
JIO Technologies LLC (Abu Dhabi, Al Muroor Road; serves Dubai, Abu Dhabi and the wider GCC) sells integrated IT and energy solutions. The website exists to convince buyers the company is a credible, established partner and to turn visits into quote requests and consultations.

## Positioning
A single supplier covering both IT (managed services, cybersecurity, data centers, networking, cloud) and power/energy (UPS, VRLA/lithium batteries, renewables), plus CCTV and 24/7 support, backed by major vendor partnerships. Few competitors cover IT and energy under one roof.

## Operating Context
Visitors compare vendors against procurement criteria: services, vendor authorisations, industry experience, support coverage. Conversion paths: "Get a Quote" / "Schedule a Free Consultation & Quote" form (3-step process: schedule call, discovery meeting, proposal), phone +971 553 440 144, WhatsApp, sales@jiotechuae.com. Existing pages: Home, Services (solutions), Company (about), Industries, Blog, Resources/FAQ, Contact, Client Support, Careers (ADP portal).

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Services: Managed IT & Infrastructure; IT Consulting & Digital Transformation; Cybersecurity & Data Protection; Data Center Design & Hosting; Power & Energy Solutions; Network & Connectivity; also CCTV, cloud/hybrid, maintenance and 24/7 support, IT procurement and vendor management.
- Industries: government and public sector, construction and engineering, healthcare, banking and finance, consulting, retail and e-commerce, energy and utilities, education and research, non-profit.
- Vendor partners shown: Dell, HPE, Lenovo, Supermicro, Intel, VMware, Microsoft, Eaton, APC, Vertiv, BPC, Cisco, Fortinet, Hikvision, Aruba, Synology and printer brands (Xerox, Epson, HP, Canon, Brother).

- Must be editable in WordPress.

## Evidence on Hand
- Real company facts, service and industry lists, vendor logo list, and contact details from the live site.
- Testimonials on the live site are anonymous (role and sector only) and unverified; do not fabricate named clients, case studies, certifications or metrics.
- No photography of projects, installations or the team has been provided.

## Product Principles
- Credibility before flair: every claim shown must be one the company can substantiate.
- One company, two strengths: IT and energy are presented as a single integrated offer, not two unrelated catalogues.
- Make the next step obvious: a quote or consultation is reachable from every page.
- Organise for procurement scanning: services, industries and vendor partners must be findable in seconds.
